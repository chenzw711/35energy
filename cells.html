LAYER STACK · SCHEMATIC

Amorphous silicon passivation on both faces is why the temperature coefficient stays flat and why the rear side generates as well as it does. It is also why the process runs cooler than a diffusion furnace — with a lower embodied carbon figure as a result.

Process advantages

Three levers, pulled at once.

Most HJT cost reduction stories pick one lever and stall on the others. Our line runs all three concurrently, which is what makes 26%+ at 100 µm economic rather than a lab result.

LEVER 01

Wafer thinning

100 µm in volume today. The roadmap continues to 65 µm for P-type heterojunction, with 30 µm processes held as intellectual property rather than production.

100 µm shipping 65 µm qualified

LEVER 02

Busbar-free metallisation

0BB removes the busbars entirely. Roughly 30% less silver paste per cell, more active area, and a shading profile that suits fine-grid printing on thin wafers.

−30% Ag paste Fine-grid screen

LEVER 03

Precious-metal reduction

20% silver-coated copper paste is already scaled. Pure-copper paste and indium-free rear targets are in qualification — the two remaining cost lines in the BOM.

20% Ag-Cu scaled Cu paste qualifying

Roadmap

What is shipping, what is qualified, what is research.

We separate these three deliberately. A technology roadmap is only useful to a buyer if it is clear which items can be ordered today.
