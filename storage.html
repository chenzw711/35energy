Product range

Where it goes

Four situations where a cabinet beats a rack.

Rack batteries are cheaper per kWh and better at scale. A cabinet wins where there is no room, no rack and nobody on site with a torque wrench.

01

Retail and small C&I

Peak shaving behind the meter where the electrical room is already full. One cabinet, one wall circuit.

02

PV self-consumption

Store the midday surplus instead of exporting it at a negative price. Pairs with our own modules on the same site.

03

Weak-grid backup

Where the outage is measured in hours, not seconds. 200 A discharge carries a real load, not just a router.

04

Telecom and remote sites

Delivered assembled. No on-site cell stacking, no busbar torquing, no specialist labour.

System thinking

We would rather size the system than sell a box.

A cabinet on its own is a component. What most projects actually need is a PV array, a storage unit and a controller that agree with each other — and a single party accountable when they do not.

  • 01

    Load profile first

    Send 30 days of interval data and we will size the cabinet against your actual peak, not against a rule of thumb.

  • 02

    Inverter compatibility

    RS485/CAN with selectable protocols. Tell us the inverter model before you order — protocol matching after delivery is expensive.

  • 03

    One interface

    When the array and the cabinet come from us, the warranty question has one answer instead of two.

Send us the load profile.

Peak demand, daily cycle, backup hours required, inverter model. We will come back with a cabinet size and an honest note if storage is the wrong answer for your tariff.
