1. Privacy notice

Who is responsible

The data controller for this website is Liansheng Technology Co., Ltd. (Shenzhen Stock Exchange code 300051), operating the international brand 35 Energy. Data protection enquiries: privacy@35energy.com.

What we collect

We collect only what is submitted through our forms or generated by serving the site:

  • Enquiry data — name, business email, company, country, role, project details and any message you write. Provided by you.
  • Document request data — business email, company and the documents requested.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, pages viewed and referring source, if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Consent records — your cookie choices, stored locally in your browser, so we can demonstrate compliance.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. We do not collect special-category personal data. Our forms are for business contacts, not consumers.

How long we keep it

  • Unsuccessful enquiries: 24 months from last contact, then deleted.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it on our instructions — website hosting, email delivery and CRM — under written data processing agreements. Where a provider is located outside the EEA, transfers rely on an adequacy decision or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses together with supplementary technical measures.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. Write to privacy@35energy.com. We respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

Automated decisions

We do not make automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not use your enquiry data to train third-party models.

4. Supply chain statement

This statement is made in relation to section 54 of the UK Modern Slavery Act 2015 and in support of our obligations under the EU Forced Labour Regulation (EU) 2024/3015, applicable from 14 December 2027.

Our structure and supply chain

We manufacture heterojunction solar cells and modules in Sichuan Province, China. Our upstream supply chain comprises polysilicon, wafer, target material, paste, encapsulant, glass, frame and connector suppliers, predominantly located in China.

Our policy

  • We prohibit forced, bonded, indentured and prison labour, and any recruitment fee charged to workers.
  • We do not knowingly source from entities named on the UFLPA entity list or subject to equivalent restrictive measures in other jurisdictions.
  • Suppliers must declare the origin of polysilicon at ingot level and notify us of any change of source.
  • Suppliers must grant audit access and accept the obligations in our supplier code of conduct.

Due diligence and remediation

Supplier onboarding includes an origin declaration, a labour-standards questionnaire and a contractual right to audit. Batch-level documentation is maintained to link finished modules to cell, wafer and polysilicon batches. Where a supplier cannot document origin, we suspend new orders pending resolution and require a corrective action plan before resuming.

Limits of this statement

This statement describes our policy and management approach. It is not an independent audit and does not certify any specific batch. Batch-level traceability files, third-party audit reports and certificates are released to customers and their auditors on request under confidentiality agreement.
