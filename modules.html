Where it wins

Lower balance-of-system cost per kWp on constrained roofs. Higher power density means fewer panels, fewer rails, fewer clamps and fewer roof penetrations for the same installed capacity.

Two-person handling. The utility format needs mechanical lifting on most commercial jobs. A compact format keeps installation in the hands of a normal crew, which matters where crane access is billable by the hour.

Same degradation curve. Identical cell platform, identical 1% first-year and 0.4%/yr linear profile — so a mixed portfolio does not create two different yield assumptions in your financial model.

If your project is a residential retrofit, tell us — we will point you to a partner product rather than sell you a panel your installers will refuse to carry.
