35 Energy is the international brand of Liansheng Technology — a Shenzhen-listed manufacturer that builds heterojunction cells and modules and nothing else.

Who we are

A single-line bet on heterojunction.

Most Chinese cell manufacturers run heterojunction as an experiment alongside a TOPCon line. We do not. Our Meishan facility is built around one process, which is why wafer thinning, 0BB metallisation and paste reduction have moved from pilot to volume at the same time rather than one at a time.

Liansheng Technology Co., Ltd. was established in 2023 and is listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange under code 300051. The company supplies cells to module manufacturers and builds its own modules under the 35 Energy brand for projects and distribution outside China.

International revenue reached 28.69% of the total in 2025, up from 12.88% in 2024. That shift is the reason this site exists: the export business has outgrown the ability to run it out of a domestic Chinese-language website.

ESG & supply chain

The part European buyers read first.

Forced-labour traceability, carbon footprint and chain of custody now decide whether a module can enter the EU at all. Here is our position and our method.

01 / ORIGIN

Polysilicon traceability

We require polysilicon suppliers to declare origin at the ingot level and to support batch-level documentation back through wafer, cell and module. Supplier onboarding includes an origin declaration and a contractual obligation to notify us of any change of source.

Request our statement

02 / LABOUR

Forced labour: zero tolerance

Our supplier code prohibits forced, bonded and prison labour and any recruitment fee charged to workers. Suppliers are required to accept audit access. We do not source from entities named on the UFLPA entity list or subject to equivalent measures in other jurisdictions.

Supplier code

03 / CARBON

Product-level footprint

Thin wafers and a sub-200 °C process reduce embodied energy before any offset is considered. We commission product-level assessments per series and release the methodology alongside the result, so a buyer can check whether it matches the method their tender requires.

Quality

What the gate actually checks.

Cell and module lines run in-line EL, IV sorting and appearance inspection. Finished modules are flash-tested and power-binned before packing, with the measurement file archived against the serial number.

  • 01

    Incoming wafer and paste control

    Thickness, resistivity and paste batch verified on receipt, not after lamination.

  • 02

    In-line EL after metallisation

    Every cell imaged. Defective cells are pulled before they reach a module.

  • 03

    Flash test and binning

    Finished modules measured and sorted to the bin on the label, with the data file archived by serial number.

  • 04

    Retained samples

    Production samples retained per batch for the warranty period, so a claim can be checked against the actual unit rather than a data sheet.
